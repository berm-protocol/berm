Archive the proof

This is the step everyone skips and the one that matters most. Your proof post dies with your X account — so the evidence disappears at exactly the moment someone else has your old handle and is claiming to be you. An archived copy is held by a third party with no stake in the fight.

A Wayback tab opens and captures the post. Leave it open until it finishes — this page polls until the snapshot appears, then records the exact URL and capture time.

Sign and publish

Two events: a standard NIP-39 claim any Nostr client understands, and an XOnly attestation carrying the archived proof and your account id.

Handles recycle; numeric ids never do. If your account is ever deleted, someone can register your handle — and a claim bound only to the handle cannot tell you apart from them. Find yours by searching “find my X user id”; it is public.
