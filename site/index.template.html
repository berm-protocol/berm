Your key, your name, your writing.

A Nostr identity you actually hold, a place to write under it, and publishing that does not depend on anyone's permission — including ours.

Use it

Signer → Make an identity and keep the only copy. Encrypted with your passphrase, downloaded to your machine, never stored here. Editor → Write once and publish to Nostr as a long-form article, to your own node, or as a draft for X. Declining publishes nothing.

If you already have a signer extension or a remote signer, use it. Those are better than anything a website can offer you, and the editor will prefer them automatically. Ours exists for people who have neither.

Check it

Who →Resolve an identity and see what is claimed versus what is verified. Import →Bring a following graph across, and see exactly what moves. Passkey check →Whether your device can derive a key from a passkey, tested in your own browser.

Understand it

Documentation →What a berm is, why identity has to be a key rather than an account, and what none of this can promise you.

Where you stand

Nothing here holds your key but your own device. There is no account, no reset, and no support address that can recover a lost passphrase — that is the cost of nobody else being able to sign as you.

This is early software. It is public so it can be checked, not because it is finished.
