Enrol a second device

Passkeys sync inside an ecosystem, so a broken phone is survivable. An iPhone plus a Windows PC is not — that is the gap a second credential closes. Both unwrap the same identity.

Download an encrypted backup

The only unrecoverable loss in the system. Articles, follows and pages all come back from relays; the key does not. Store this away from your devices.

Name guardians

If your key is ever lost, guardians can attest that a new one is you. This must be published before the loss — a pre-commitment added afterwards proves nothing, which is exactly when you would want it.

Something already happened
What guardians can and cannot do

There is no account to recover. Your identity is your key. Guardians cannot restore it — they can only attest that a new key is the same person. That is social consensus, not cryptography, and it works because relying parties choose to honour it.

Removing a device does not revoke it. The identity key is unchanged, so a kept copy of the blob plus that authenticator still unwraps it. Real revocation means rotating to a new key.
