Act II — the fix, without the seed phrase

Touch the sensor. That's the whole onboarding.

Your key comes from a WebAuthn PRF: 32 bytes computed inside your authenticator, bound to that credential, never transmitted. Nostr's adoption problem was never ideology — it was twelve words on a piece of paper. This removes them without handing custody to anyone.
